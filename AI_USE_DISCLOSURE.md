## Representative prompts

1. To Claude: "i am testing this venture idea: movie reccomendation app based on your fav books & songs ... define a prototype that can test this assumption in 20 minutes." This produced a one-user, one-action, three-feature prototype plan. I kept the three-feature scope and the exclusion of accounts and genre filters.
2. To Replit (revision): a three-part prompt asking for cleared stale results with inline messages, "Try another" after "No" and "Already seen it," and a confidence label with notes on unfamiliar inputs. It produced all three. I changed the plan after testing because of the problems below.
3. To Replit (correction): "it doesnt incorporate the 2nd and 3rd choices at all now. the reasoning is only based on the 1st input fix that." This led to a fix so the reason uses all three inputs.

## Suggestions I rejected or changed

- Claude suggested having the recommendation prompt name "the single input that drove the match." After I added it in Replit, the reasoning used only my first input and ignored the second and third. I told Replit to require all three inputs in the reason and made the driving input optional.
- Claude suggested warning when an input "looks like a film, not a book or song." I caught that many works are both books and films, so the warning would be wrong. I told Replit to remove it and only note unfamiliar or ambiguous inputs.

## Where my own judgment mattered

- Choosing the three improvements to make and which feedback to defer, such as Reviewer 3's taste-profile idea, which didn't fit my scope.
- Noticing the first-input-only bug and the book-versus-film problem, which the AI had not.
- Deciding how to read the test results honestly, including that the "Strong match" label was overconfident in one case.

## AI inside the product

- **Task:** given three favorites, recommend one film and explain the match
- **Inputs:** three short text strings (books, songs, or artists)
- **Outputs:** film title, year, genre, a reason that uses all three inputs, a Strong/Loose confidence label, and notes on unfamiliar or ambiguous inputs
- **Status:** Live model call. OpenAI through Replit's managed AI integration, model `gpt-5.6-luna` as written in the code. The prompt is `MOVIE_PROMPT` in `artifacts/api-server/src/lib/movie-recommendation.ts`, used in `artifacts/api-server/src/routes/taste.ts`.
- **Reaction buttons:** stored only in the user's browser.

## What I'm not fully sure about

- I have not verified that every recommended film exists in a movie database.
- The recommendations are generated by a live model, so I cannot promise they are accurate or consistent.
- Some wording in these documents was drafted with AI help, and I edited it to match what I actually did and saw.
